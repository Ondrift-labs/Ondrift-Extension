# Ondrift refund policy

English | [한국어](docs/REFUND.ko.md) | [日本語](docs/REFUND.ja.md) | [简体中文](docs/REFUND.zh.md)

Effective date: August 25, 2026

This policy covers the Ondrift Pro subscription ($2.99/month), billed and
processed by Paddle.com Market Limited, our authorized reseller and merchant
of record.

## Cancel anytime

You can cancel your Ondrift Pro subscription at any time. Cancelling stops
future renewals; you keep Pro access (100 rewrites/day) until the end of the
billing period you already paid for. No partial refund is issued for the
unused remainder of a billing period unless required by law or granted under
this policy.

## Refund eligibility

We will issue a full refund, no questions asked, if you request one within 7
days of your **first** Ondrift Pro payment.

Refund requests for a renewal charge, or for a first payment made outside the
7-day window, are considered case by case — for example, if Ondrift Pro was
charged but never issued a working license, or a renewal charge went through
after you had already cancelled.

## How to request a refund

Refund requests are handled by Paddle. Use the link in your payment receipt
email from Paddle, or contact the verified publisher/support channel listed
on Ondrift's Chrome Web Store listing with your order or transaction ID.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method and typically
appear within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.
